Perfect Timing Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

Perfect Timing is published by Unfazed Action. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Perfect Timing mobile application.

Information stored by Perfect Timing

Perfect Timing does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information to play the game.

Certain game information and preferences, such as your best score, sound setting, and haptic-feedback setting, may be stored locally on your device so that these preferences and progress can be maintained between sessions.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Perfect Timing uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information used for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Depending on the user's device, region, privacy choices, and applicable law, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • interactions with the app and advertisements; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their respective privacy practices.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Perfect Timing uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy choices and obtain consent before requesting advertising in accordance with the user's consent status.

Users in applicable regions may be provided options regarding personalized advertising, data processing, and other privacy choices. Available choices may vary based on location and applicable law.

Data sharing

Perfect Timing does not operate its own server or user-account system for the purpose of collecting personal information.

Information may, however, be collected, processed, or shared by third-party services integrated into the app, including Google AdMob, as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention services.

Children's privacy

Perfect Timing is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. The app's intended audience and advertising configuration will be declared through the applicable app stores and configured in accordance with their requirements.

If Perfect Timing is made available to children or includes children within its declared target audience in the future, Unfazed Action will apply the additional privacy and advertising protections required for those users.

Data security

Perfect Timing itself keeps game preferences such as best score and settings locally on the user's device. Information processed by third-party services is subject to the security practices and policies of those providers.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored Perfect Timing game data remains on the device unless it is cleared through the device's application settings or the application is removed, subject to the device operating system's backup and restoration behavior.

Information processed by Google or other advertising partners is retained according to those providers' applicable policies and the user's privacy settings.

Third-party services

Perfect Timing uses Google AdMob and related Google services. More information about how Google handles information from apps that use Google services is available through Google's privacy and advertising documentation.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Perfect Timing changes, additional features or services are introduced, or legal and platform requirements change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when appropriate.
