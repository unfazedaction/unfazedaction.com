Frequently asked questions.

How does my UA Account work?

Your UA Account is your identity across Unfazed Action. Select Sign In on the public website and use your existing account to enter the UA member area, view account information and open Second Brain. New account registration, password recovery and full account management are not yet available on the website. Never share your password.

Why are my Second Brain reminders not sending notifications?

Web reminders are stored in the workspace. Web push notifications are not enabled.

How can I export Second Brain data?

In the signed-in workspace, use Export my data (JSON) in the Explore sidebar. Keep exported files private.

Can I purchase UA+ or UA+ Family?

Not yet. Paid memberships and connected intelligence are planned offerings, not currently purchasable services.

Where can I download a Specialist?

Specialist download availability has not been announced. Visit the Specialist pages to learn about each product.

What should I do if synchronization appears interrupted?

Check your internet connection and the workspace sync status. Avoid deleting entries or clearing browser data to troubleshoot. If needed, preserve an export of accessible entries.
