The idea

Your home already has systems. HomeForge helps you see them.

Food, supplies, chores, shopping, shared responsibilities, and household routines are already connected in real life. HomeForge is being designed to bring those pieces together without turning home management into another complicated job.

Know what you have

Track household inventory so important supplies do not disappear unnoticed until the moment you need them.

Know what you need

Low quantities can feed directly into shopping lists, helping the household move from awareness to action.

Keep the household connected

Shared members, family roles, and future child profiles can help everyone participate appropriately.

Reduce wasted effort

Expiration awareness, room organization, and connected meal planning can help use what is already in the home.

Planned core features

The HomeForge toolkit.

The product direction centers on practical household tools that can work together instead of living in separate lists and apps.

Future Connection
Connected household

Inventory becomes more useful when it connects to action.

HomeForge is not just meant to be a digital list of items. The value comes from using household information to drive shopping, meal planning, reminders, and shared routines.

Inventory → Shopping

Low quantities can become shopping needs without starting another list from scratch.

Inventory → Meals

Available food can eventually support MealForge planning and suggestions.

Home → Family

Shared household information can be visible to the right family members through UA Account roles.

Family-ready

Designed around the household, not just one phone.

HomeForge is intended to work naturally with UA Family so adults and managed child profiles can participate in the home at the right level.

Shared household view

Members can work from the same household information rather than maintaining disconnected personal lists.

Managed child profiles

Parent-authorized child accounts can eventually support shared chores and age-appropriate household tasks.

Invitations

Adult household members can join through the existing UA Family invitation system.

UA+ family access

Eligible UA+ family entitlements can extend supported premium HomeForge features across the household.
