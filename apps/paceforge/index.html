PACEFORGE

Every Step Has a Purpose.

PaceForge is being built as a purposeful running platform for athletes who want more than a stopwatch. It combines pace guidance, structured workouts, interval training, workout history, and future coaching intelligence inside the Unfazed Action ecosystem.

The idea

Running tools that do more than record a run.

PaceForge is designed around the idea that every workout should have a purpose. Whether someone is building fitness, learning to pace themselves, running intervals, or training toward a race goal, the app should help them understand what they are doing while they are doing it.

Guidance while you run

PaceForge is designed to actively support the workout, not simply display statistics after it is over.

Structure without unnecessary complexity

Powerful workout tools should still feel clear and approachable when someone is standing at the starting line ready to move.

Your history should matter

Completed workouts can eventually help PaceForge provide better recommendations instead of becoming forgotten entries in a log.

Part of something larger

PaceForge is a specialized running app, while UA Account and UA+ connect it to the broader Unfazed Action ecosystem.

Core features

The PaceForge toolkit.

The foundation is already centered around guided running, customizable workouts, saved training, and a growing workout history.

PC

Pace Coach

Real-time pace guidance designed to help runners stay near a target pace using live coaching cues, pace smoothing, thresholds, GPS acquisition logic, and workout controls.

Core Feature
IN

Interval Training

Build fully customizable interval sessions with warmups, cooldowns, work and recovery blocks, multiple rounds, voice cues, pause/resume, and skip controls.

Core Feature
SW

Saved Workouts

Create workouts once and reuse them when it is time to train, including saved names and structured spoken workout cues.

Core Feature
WH

Workout History

Completed workouts are stored as part of a cloud-aware history system with local fallback, deletion controls, and future progress analysis.

Growing Feature
TM

Treadmill Mode

Pace Coach will eventually support indoor treadmill running using a different pace source than GPS, beginning with manual treadmill speed or pace entry and potentially connected sensors later.

Planned
TP

Training Pace Recommendations

A future coach-assisted system can use completed comparable workouts to recommend updates to easy, tempo, 5K, 10K, and long-run training paces while keeping the runner in control of every change.

Planned
Future roadmap

PaceForge is designed to keep growing.

The long-term vision is broader than the first release. New workout types and connected training tools can be added without losing the simple core experience.

Workout Types

More structured running modes

Future modules can include Couch to 5K, Long Run, Tempo Run, Race Day, Track Workout, and Custom Workout experiences.

Wearables

Watch support

PaceForge is intended to expand beyond the phone, including support for compatible wearable devices such as Galaxy Watch.

Coaching

Smarter use of completed workouts

Workout history can eventually help power training insights and recommendations while requiring enough evidence and user approval before changing saved training targets.

Progress

Better history and plan summaries

Advanced workout filtering and plan-specific progress summaries are planned after higher-priority launch and home experience work is complete.

Unfazed Action

PaceForge uses UA Account. It does not own it.

PaceForge is designed to stay focused on running. UA Account, UA+ status, family membership, and broader account management belong to the Unfazed Action ecosystem. Over time, PaceForge should simply show the runner's UA status and provide a clear path back to the central UA Account website for management.

Platforms

Built to reach runners on both major mobile platforms.

PaceForge development currently centers on Android, with an iPhone release included in the broader product roadmap.

Android

The current PaceForge build is being developed in Android Studio using Kotlin, including foreground workout services, notifications, audio cues, Firebase, and UA Account integration.

iPhone

PaceForge is intended to reach the Apple App Store as well. Cross-platform planning will continue as the Android product foundation matures.
