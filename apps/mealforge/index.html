MEALFORGE

Make food work for your life.

MealForge is being built to make meal planning, meal prep, recipes, grocery decisions, and household food needs easier to manage. It stays focused on food while using relevant UA context to make smarter recommendations when the user wants that connection.

The idea

Meal planning should fit the people, not the other way around.

MealForge is being designed around the reality that meals have to work for actual households: schedules, preferences, nutrition goals, available ingredients, prep time, and the people sitting at the table.

Plan with purpose

Build meal plans around household needs instead of starting from a blank calendar every week.

Make prep easier

Turn planned meals into practical prep steps so cooking does not become another source of last-minute stress.

Use context intelligently

Relevant UA context can help MealForge understand what is already available and what the household actually needs.

Reduce waste and duplicate buying

Better planning can help use available food before buying more of the same thing or letting ingredients expire unused.

MealForge should understand more than a recipe title.

The broader UA ecosystem can give MealForge the context it needs to make better decisions while each app stays focused on its own job. MealForge plans food. HomeForge manages the home. Other UA apps contribute context when it is relevant.

Built for real households
