Every UA app is designed to focus on a specific part of life while connecting back to one larger ecosystem. Train. Organize. Eat better. Build habits. Get stronger. Understand your money. Strengthen your mindset.

The UA approach

Specialized apps.
Shared purpose.

Unfazed Action is not being built as one giant app trying to do everything. Each Forge app is designed to solve its own problem well while sharing the same UA identity, account system, family structure, and broader mission.

One UA Account

The long-term goal is simple: sign into Unfazed Action once and carry your UA identity across the ecosystem. Your apps remain specialized while your experience becomes connected.

The long-term vision

Apps that understand they are part of something bigger.

The future of Unfazed Action is not just a collection of apps. It is an ecosystem where specialized tools can work together—with the user's permission—to provide a more complete view of their goals, routines, household, training, food, finances, and personal growth.
