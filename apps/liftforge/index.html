The idea

Strength training should make progress easier to see.

LiftForge is being built around structured training, understandable progression, and useful history so users can focus on the work in front of them while still seeing how far they have come.

Train with structure

Build and follow workouts with clear exercises, sets, reps, rest, and training intent.

Understand progression

See whether strength, volume, consistency, and exercise performance are moving in the right direction.

Use history, not guesswork

Previous sessions can help users choose weights and targets based on what they have actually done.

Stay connected to the bigger UA picture

Relevant context from across UA can make strength training more useful without turning LiftForge into a general-purpose life app.

Built around progression

The goal is not just to log workouts. It is to understand them.

LiftForge should make it easy to see what changed between sessions and what that means for the next one. Logging is useful only when it helps users make better training decisions.

Session context

Know what happened last time before deciding what should happen today.

Meaningful progression

Progress can come through load, reps, volume, control, consistency, or stronger execution.

Clear next steps

The app should help users understand where to go next without pretending every workout needs to be a personal record.
